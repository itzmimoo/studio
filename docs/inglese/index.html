Reported speech

Cos'è

Quando racconti cosa ha detto qualcun altro hai due modi:

Discorso diretto

Ripeti le parole esatte, tra virgolette.

Tom said, "I like pizza."

Discorso indiretto (reported speech)

Racconti il senso, senza virgolette, e cambi alcune parole.

Tom said (that) he liked pizza.

Come si fa: 4 passi

PassoCosa faiEsempio
1Togli le virgolette e usa said / told (+ that, facoltativo).He said that …
2Cambia il pronome (chi parla non è più "io")."I am tired" → She said she was tired
3Fai un passo indietro nel tempo col verbo (backshift).am → was, like → liked
4Cambia le espressioni di tempo e luogo.tomorrow → the next day

Esempio completo

DirettoAnna: "I am tired today."
RiportatoAnna said (that) she was tired that day.
Il trucco: chiediti sempre chi parla e quando lo racconto. Se racconti dopo, il verbo va indietro.

Il verbo fa un passo indietro nel passato.

Frase direttaFrase riportataEsempio
Present simplePast simple"I like tea" → he said he liked tea
Present continuousPast continuous"I am working" → she said she was working
Present perfectPast perfect"I have finished" → he said he had finished
Past simplePast perfect"I saw him" → she said she had seen him
Past continuousPast perfect continuous"I was sleeping" → he said he had been sleeping
willwould"I will call" → she said she would call
cancould"I can swim" → he said he could swim
maymight"It may rain" → she said it might rain
musthad to"I must go" → he said he had to go
am/is/are going towas/were going to"I'm going to study" → she said she was going to study
Non cambiano: would, could, should, might, ought to e il past perfect (hanno già la forma "passata").

Esempi

Diretto"I'll see you tomorrow," he said.
RiportatoHe said he would see me the next day.
Diretto"I was in London last week," she said.
RiportatoShe said she had been in London the week before.
Diretto"I like this book," he said.
RiportatoHe said he liked that book.
Se parli lo stesso giorno o nello stesso posto, non cambiarli: "I'm tired today" → (stesso giorno) She says she's tired today.

Say e tell

SAY

said + (that)

She said (that) she was happy.

Con la persona: said to me

TELL

told + persona + (that)

She told me (that) she was happy.

Dopo tell c'è sempre la persona.

Errore tipico

✗ She said me that…   ✓ She told me that… / She said that…

Quando NON fai il backshift

  • Il verbo che introduce è al presente: "I'm tired" → He says he is tired.
  • Quello che dice è una verità sempre valida: The teacher said that the Earth goes round the Sun.
  • C'è già would, could, should, might, ought to o past perfect.
  • La cosa è ancora vera adesso (facoltativo): "I live in Rome" → She said she lives / lived in Rome.

Bonus: ordini e richieste

tell / ask + persona + to + verbo
Diretto"Close the door," he said.
RiportatoHe told me to close the door.
Diretto"Please help me," she said.
RiportatoShe asked me to help her.
Diretto"Don't be late," Mum said.
RiportatoMum told me not to be late.

Reported questions

La regola d'oro

Una domanda riportata non è più una domanda. Diventa una frase normale (soggetto + verbo).

NIENTE
  • punto interrogativo ?
  • inversione (are you, do you)
  • do / does / did
SI USA
  • asked (+ persona)
  • if / whether oppure la wh-word
  • ordine: soggetto + verbo
  • backshift e pronomi come prima

Prima e dopo

Diretto"Where do you live?"
RiportatoHe asked where I lived.
Diretto"Are you tired?"
RiportatoShe asked if I was tired.
Immagina la domanda come una frase affermativa: "Where you live" → "where I lived".
Domande sì/no

Cominciano con un ausiliare (do, are, have, can…). Si riporta con if o whether (stesso significato).

Schema
asked (me) + if / whether + soggetto + verbo
Diretto"Do you like pizza?"
RiportatoHe asked me if I liked pizza.
Diretto"Are you tired?"
RiportatoShe asked if I was tired.
Diretto"Have you finished?"
RiportatoThe teacher asked whether I had finished.
Diretto"Can you swim?"
RiportatoHe asked me if I could swim.
Diretto"Did you call Tom?"
RiportatoShe asked if I had called Tom.
Gli ausiliari do / does / did spariscono: servivano solo a formare la domanda.
Domande con wh-

Cominciano con what, where, when, why, who, how…. La wh-word resta, poi ordine normale.

Schema
asked (me) + wh-word + soggetto + verbo
Diretto"Where do you live?"
RiportatoHe asked me where I lived.
Diretto"What are you doing?"
RiportatoShe asked what I was doing.
Diretto"Why did you leave?"
RiportatoHe asked why I had left.
Diretto"What time does the film start?"
RiportatoShe asked what time the film started.
Diretto"How old are you?"
RiportatoHe asked how old I was.
Caso speciale: who / what è il soggetto

Se la wh-word è il soggetto non c'è inversione nemmeno nel diretto: cambia solo il tempo.

Diretto"Who called you?"
RiportatoHe asked who had called me.

Errori da evitare

ErroreSbagliatoGiusto

Verbi che puoi usare

asked (me)

Il più comune.

wanted to know

She wanted to know where I lived.

wondered

He wondered if I was tired.

Esercizi

Prova a riscrivere la frase, poi tocca per vedere la risposta.

1. "I am tired," said Anna.

Anna said (that) she was tired.

am → was, I → she

2. "We play tennis every Sunday," Mark said.

Mark said (that) they played tennis every Sunday.

play → played, we → they

3. "I have finished my homework," Lucy told me.

Lucy told me (that) she had finished her homework.

present perfect → past perfect

4. "I will call you tomorrow," he said.

He said (that) he would call me the next day.

will → would, you → me, tomorrow → the next day

5. "I saw that film last week," Tom said.

Tom said (that) he had seen that film the week before.

past simple → past perfect, last week → the week before

6. "I can't come to the party," she said.

She said (that) she couldn't come to the party.

can → could

Ricorda: niente inversione, niente do/did, niente punto interrogativo.

1. "Do you like pizza?" he asked me.

He asked me if / whether I liked pizza.

domanda sì/no → if, do sparisce

2. "Where do you live?" she asked.

She asked where I lived.

where resta, ordine normale

3. "Have you finished?" the teacher asked.

The teacher asked if I had finished.

present perfect → past perfect

4. "What are you doing?" Sam asked.

Sam asked what I was doing.

are → was, you → I

5. "Why did you leave early?" Mum asked.

Mum asked why I had left early.

did sparisce, past simple → past perfect

6. "Can you help me?" she asked him.

She asked him if he could help her.

can → could, you → he, me → her

7. "Where is the station?" a tourist asked.

A tourist asked where the station was.

is → was; soggetto prima del verbo
